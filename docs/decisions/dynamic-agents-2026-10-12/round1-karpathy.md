### Essential Question
Which routing decisions can Diamond learn reliably enough to increase independently accepted changes per cost?

### Capability Assessment
**Facts (BRIEF, findings 2–3, 8, 12):** No representative dataset exists; recurrence is uncalibrated, routing disconnected, native dynamic execution unvalidated.
**Inference:** A (fixed routes) is the cheapest baseline; B (constrained composition) offers testable specialization; C (free-form generation/online learning) offers discovery but changes the action space continually.

### Training Dynamics
**Recommendation:** Require digest-verified, project-scoped outcomes; success needs trusted tests and independent delivery acceptance. Retain failures and all costs; censor unresolved outcomes. Reviewer assertions are not labels. Group retries, descendants, and near-duplicates by lineage before splitting training/holdouts; reserve future-task evaluation.

### Build vs. Prompt vs. Don't
Pilot deterministic B against A; skip fine-tuning. Controller maintainer owns paired isolated-snapshot evaluations, randomized execution order, blinded acceptance, and separate composition/routing ablations. Log selection probabilities: deterministic historical logs cannot establish unchosen-route counterfactuals.

### Failure Modes
Version model/CLI/profile/tools/evaluator; quarantine stale negative memory and requalify after drift. Otherwise learning rewards easy-task selection or obsolete failures.

### Verdict
**Proposed gate:** Adapt only when a preregistered 95% lower confidence bound exceeds a minimum worthwhile accepted-changes/cost gain across two lineage-disjoint temporal holdouts, without quality or p95-latency regression. Prespecify sample size and dollar/token/time/retry caps, including review/exploration. Inconclusive at budget exhaustion means A. Keep C experimental.

### Confidence
**Medium. Assumptions:** repeatable sandboxes, independent acceptance. **Unknowns:** task volume, effect size, model identities, affordable budgets.

### Where I May Be Wrong
**Objection:** B’s maintenance cost could erase specialization gains. **Falsification test:** If its budget-capped pilot fails the gate after amortizing maintenance, reject B’s adoption and retain A.
