### Essential Question
Which feedback structure turns Diamond’s evidence into durable useful changes?

### System Map
**Facts (BRIEF.md, items 2,7–9):** analyzer lacks outcome persistence; routing is disconnected; self-improvement publication bypasses delivery gates; health logic imposes 300-second success cooldown.
**Inference (causal-loop-mapping):** stocks include unresolved failures, candidate profiles, and pending production outcomes.
**R1, success-to-the-successful:** favorable evidence +→ candidate rank +→ route exposure +→ counted successes +→ favorable evidence.
**B1, proposed correction:** scoped unresolved-failure evidence +→ corrective candidate priority +→ authorized route trials +→ durable accepted results −→ scoped unresolved-failure evidence.

### Leverage Point Analysis
**Inference:** A repairs evidence cheaply and supplies a baseline, but preserves capability mismatches. B’s constrained profiles/deterministic routing permit attributable adaptation, at maintenance cost. C’s free-form teams/online learning offer broader exploration but amplify R1 and confound attribution.
Priority: durable-utility goal, evidence/authorization rules, then thresholds.

### Unintended Consequences
**Inference:** delayed regressions reward bad routes prematurely. Negative memory risks permanent exclusion despite version improvements: retain failures, append resolutions, scope by project/task/profile/model/version, and allow bounded requalification.

### The Structural Fix
**Proposal:** controller owner links evidence IDs, candidate digest, frozen route/profile, and result IDs; independent evaluator appends acceptance, cost/time, and delayed regressions. Hysteresis uses distinct switch/return thresholds and minimum dwell, bypassed when eligibility fails; each switch requires reauthorization. Success cooldown instead excludes a healthy incumbent.

### Verdict
A immediately; B through matched holdouts; C lacks justification today.

### Confidence

### Where I May Be Wrong

**Falsification test:** evaluator preregisters equal-budget A/B trials and an outcome window; reject B if matured useful-change yield per cost/time, including maintenance, fails to beat A.
